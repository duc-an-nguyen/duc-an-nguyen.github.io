Who I am

An engineer and researcher focused on efficient, trustworthy AI.

I am Duc An Nguyen, a Computer Vision and Edge AI Engineer with hands-on experience developing and deploying deep-learning systems across resource-constrained devices and server infrastructure.

My work sits at the intersection of computer vision, embedded computing, model optimization, and explainable artificial intelligence. I enjoy taking an idea from research and turning it into a reliable system that can operate under real constraints: limited memory, limited computation, noisy data, and real-time performance requirements.

I have developed vision systems for industrial sorting, embedded fingerprint recognition, smart-city surveillance, neural-signal analysis, and system-health monitoring. Across these projects, my goal has remained the same: create AI that is technically strong, efficient to deploy, and understandable to the people who use it.
